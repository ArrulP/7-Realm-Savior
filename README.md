# 7 Realm Savior

A rogue-like action game where you choose a character, battle through 7 dying realms, collect their hearts, and defeat the Dominator of the Night.

## Lore

Dominator Noctis conquered the 7 realms and drains their life to fuel his own world. Each realm's heart — a gemstone of immense power — was sent back as an anchor for his spell.

Take the hearts. Tear open the path to his world. End his reign.

## Gameplay

- Choose a character (each with unique abilities)
- 7 realms in random order per run
- Collect a realm's heart → portal to next realm
- All 7 hearts → portal to Noctis's world
- Destroy his spawner pillars → face the realm-swapping final boss

## Items

- Random drops
- Combine items into higher tiers
- Cursed items (strong with drawbacks)
- Character-specific interactions

## Status

Early development. Core systems in progress.
